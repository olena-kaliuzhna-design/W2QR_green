How It Works

W2QR encrypts your seed phrase into a QR code protected by your password. Every password produces a valid seed phrase — attackers can never tell which one is real. Use the Strong Password Generator to derive a quantum-resistant password from a memorable phrase and store it as a password QR. Print both QR codes on plastic cards for easy, durable access. Back up to Google Drive for extra redundancy.

Encrypting Your Seed Phrase

  1. Enter or generate your seed phrase

    Type or paste your 12- or 24-word seed phrase, or let W2QR generate one for you. W2QR checks it for validity before continuing.

  2. Set your encryption password

    Type a password directly, or use the Strong Password Generator to derive a cryptographic password from a memorable phrase you can easily remember. The generated password has over 150 bits of entropy — quantum-resistant — and can be stored as a separate password QR for convenient scanning. W2QR runs the password through Argon2id, a deliberately slow, memory-hard process that makes each guess expensive for an attacker.

  3. Your encrypted QR is created

    Your seed phrase is encrypted using honey encryption: if anyone tries a wrong password, they still get a valid-looking seed phrase with real wallet addresses. There is no way to tell the difference between the real result and a decoy. The encrypted result is packed into a compact QR code you can download as PNG, SVG, or JSON.

  4. Back up and print

    Back up your encrypted QR to Google Drive for safekeeping. Print your wallet QR and password QR on plastic cards — two credit-card-sized cards that together give you easy, durable access to your wallet.

Unlocking Your Wallet

  1. Scan or upload your QR code

    Use your camera to scan the QR, or upload the image file. W2QR detects the format automatically.

  2. Enter your password

    The same slow password processing runs entirely in your browser — no server is involved.

  3. Your seed phrase appears

    The decryption reverses the encryption process and your original seed phrase appears on screen. It exists only in your browser for as long as you keep the page open — it is never stored or sent anywhere.

Why Honey Encryption?

With traditional encryption, a wrong password produces obviously random garbage. An attacker trying passwords knows instantly when they hit the right one — the output suddenly looks like real data.

Honey encryption removes that signal entirely. Every password — right or wrong — produces a valid seed phrase that controls a real-looking wallet with real addresses. An attacker trying a million passwords gets a million plausible wallets.

To find the real one, they would have to check each wallet on the blockchain. Combined with the slow password processing that makes each attempt expensive, brute-force becomes economically pointless.

Security Summary

  • All encryption and decryption runs in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.
  • Slow, memory-intensive password processing makes each guess expensive for attackers.
  • Every wrong password produces a valid seed phrase — there is no way to tell the real result from a decoy.
  • Strong passwords derived from your memorable phrase require ~10⁴⁵ years to brute-force on classical hardware and ~175 billion years with quantum computers.
  • Optional Google Drive backup provides extra redundancy.
  • No password verifier is stored anywhere — there is nothing for an attacker to check against.

W2QR Cards

  1. Design and print your card

    Create a card with a pre-generated QR code using the Card Designer. Choose a template, add a nickname and wallet code, then print the card at CR80 (credit card) size or save it as a digital image.

  2. Activate with your password

    Scan the card QR and choose a password. Thanks to honey encryption, the card's random data combined with your password produces a unique, valid BIP-39 mnemonic — your new wallet seed phrase.

  3. Optionally back up

    After activation, back up your encrypted QR to Google Drive for extra redundancy.

Plastic Card Activation

  1. Receive your card

    You receive a physical card with a pre-printed QR code — ready to activate with just a password.

  2. Scan the QR code

    Open W2QR in your browser and scan the card with your camera, or upload a photo of it.

  3. Create a strong password

    Choose a password for your wallet. This becomes the key that protects your seed phrase.

  4. Card is activated

    Your card is now active. Optionally back up the encrypted QR to Google Drive for extra redundancy.

Strong Password & Password QR

  1. Open the Strong Password Generator

    Go to Tools → Strong Password Generator.

  2. Type a memorable phrase

    Enter a sentence you can easily remember, in any language — for example, "I was born in London, and my whole family is from that same area."

  3. W2QR derives a cryptographic password

    Your phrase is transformed into a long, complex password with over 150 bits of entropy. Brute-forcing it would take ~10⁴⁵ years on a powerful GPU cluster, or ~175 billion years even with a hypothetical quantum computer.

  4. Store the password as a QR code

    Save the generated password as a separate QR code. Print it on a plastic card and store it apart from your wallet QR — two physical pieces that must be combined to open the wallet. If you ever lose the password QR, simply regenerate it from the same phrase.
