Description of service

W2QR is a client-side application that encrypts BIP-39 seed phrases into QR codes using honey encryption.

  • Password encryption — QR + password. All encryption runs in-browser. Every password produces a valid mnemonic (honey encryption).
  • W2QR Card — A pre-printed physical or digital card with a QR code that becomes a wallet when activated with a password.

W2QR also provides a built-in wallet for viewing addresses, checking balances, and managing assets — no external wallet software is required. Optionally, W2QR can act as an external signer for MetaMask (via the W2QR Connect Snap) or other wallets (via the W2QR Remote Signer extension).

W2QR does not hold funds or manage blockchain state. It derives temporary signing keys from your encrypted QR backup during time-limited sessions.

Non-custodial disclaimer

W2QR is non-custodial. We have no access to your seed phrase, password, private keys, signing keys, or funds. We cannot recover lost passwords or QR backups. We cannot reverse, modify, or cancel any blockchain transaction.

W2QR Cards create wallets through honey encryption. W2QR cannot determine which wallet a card will produce without the user’s password.

Data handling

W2QR never receives, transmits, or stores your password, seed phrase (mnemonic), or private keys. All cryptographic operations — encryption, decryption, key derivation, and transaction signing — run entirely in your browser.

The server may store KEK-encrypted ciphertext for optional features like Google Drive backup, linked to a hashed identifier — not to your Google email, wallet address, or any personal data. This encrypted data cannot be used to access your wallet without your password.

Plastic card activation

A pre-printed plastic card contains a honey-encrypted QR code. When activated with a password, honey encryption derives a unique BIP-39 mnemonic. The card's QR is your wallet backup — scan it and enter your password to access your wallet.

Permanent loss disclaimer

If you lose all of the following simultaneously, your wallet is permanently unrecoverable and your funds will be lost:

W2QR cannot recover your wallet under any circumstances. There is no "reset password via email" or account recovery process. This is by design — it is what makes W2QR non-custodial and secure.
