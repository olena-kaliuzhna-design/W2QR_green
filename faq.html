Honey Encryption

What is honey encryption and why does W2QR use it?
  1. Honey encryption is a cryptographic technique where every possible decryption key (password) produces a valid-looking plaintext.
  2. In W2QR, every password you try yields a valid BIP-39 mnemonic — there is no "wrong password" error.
  3. An attacker who steals your QR code and brute-forces passwords gets millions of valid mnemonics, each pointing to a different wallet.
  4. The attacker has no way to know which mnemonic is yours without checking each one against the blockchain — making brute-force economically infeasible.
  5. Combined with Argon2id (64 MB memory, 3 iterations), each attempt is slow and memory-intensive, further raising the cost of attack.
How do I know the correct password worked?
  1. After decryption, W2QR shows the derived Ethereum address.
  2. Compare this address to the one you expect (your real wallet address).
  3. If the address matches, the correct password was entered.
  4. If you see an unfamiliar address with no balance, you likely entered the wrong password — re-scan and try again.
Does W2QR store a password hash or verifier?
  1. No. W2QR never stores a password hash, password-derived MAC, AEAD tag under a password-derived key, known plaintext sentinel, or wallet address.
  2. There is no way to check if a password is correct without deriving addresses and checking the blockchain.
  3. This is the core security guarantee of honey encryption.

Encryption Mode

Do I need to choose an encryption mode?
  1. No. New encrypted wallet creation uses one password-based flow.
  2. Enter your seed phrase, choose a password, and W2QR produces a honey-encrypted QR code.
  3. For maximum security, use the Strong Password Generator to derive a quantum-resistant password from a memorable phrase, and store it as a password QR.
  4. After creation you can optionally back up to Google Drive.
What changed for existing wallets?
  1. Existing non-code unlock paths remain available. Recovery codes are no longer generated or accepted.
  2. Previously created QR codes (V1, V2B) continue to work. W2QR detects the format automatically.
  3. New wallets follow the single password-based flow with optional backups.

Strong Password & Password QR

What is the Strong Password Generator?
  1. The Strong Password Generator transforms a memorable phrase you choose — in any language — into a cryptographic password with over 150 bits of entropy.
  2. For example, "I was born in London, and my whole family is from that same area" becomes a long, complex password that would take ~10⁴⁵ years to brute-force on a powerful GPU cluster.
  3. Even a hypothetical large-scale quantum computer using Grover's algorithm would need ~175 billion years — far beyond the age of the universe.
  4. The generated password is stored as a QR code that you can print. You remember the phrase; the QR carries the password.
Can I recover my password if I lose the password QR?
  1. Yes, if you generated the password from a memorable phrase. The derivation is deterministic — the same phrase always produces the same password.
  2. Open the Strong Password Generator, type the same phrase, and your password is regenerated instantly.
  3. No backup file is needed — your memory of the phrase is the backup.
Should I use a strong generated password or a regular one?
  1. A strong generated password is recommended for maximum security. It makes brute-force truly impossible, even with future quantum computers.
  2. A regular password you choose yourself is still protected by honey encryption — every password produces a valid mnemonic, so an attacker cannot confirm a guess without checking the blockchain.
  3. The difference: a weak regular password has fewer possible combinations to try. A strong generated password from a memorable phrase has over 150 bits of entropy, making exhaustive search infeasible by any known technology.

Backup & Restore

What happens if I forget my password?
  1. The password is the only decryption key. W2QR cannot reset or recover it for you — this is by design.
  2. If your password was generated with the Strong Password Generator from a memorable phrase in your native language, open the generator, re-enter the same phrase, and recover your password. The derivation is deterministic — the same phrase always produces the same password.
  3. If you chose a regular password, consider keeping a written reminder in a secure location.
Can I back up a wallet to Google Drive?
  1. Yes. Use Back Up to Google Drive on the QR result page or Tools → Cloud Backup.
  2. The backup file is encrypted ciphertext. Google Drive stores the file, but opening it still requires your password.
  3. You can restore from Google Drive on the Cloud Backup page at any time.
Does signing in to Google unlock the backed-up wallet?
  1. Drive sign-in gives access to the backup file. Opening it still requires your password.
  2. Google never receives your password, mnemonic, or private keys.
  3. The backup is ciphertext only — useless without the correct password.
What if I lose my QR code?
  1. If you backed up to Google Drive, restore the encrypted backup from the Cloud Backup page. This is equivalent to having another copy of the QR.
  2. You still need your password to decrypt. The backup is ciphertext only.

Create Encrypted Wallet

How do I create an encrypted QR code for my mnemonic?
  1. Go to Create > New Encrypted Wallet from the top menu.
  2. Enter your BIP-39 seed phrase (12 or 24 words) or generate a new one.
  3. Choose a strong password — this will be your encryption key.
  4. W2QR encrypts the mnemonic entirely in your browser using Argon2id (WebAssembly).
  5. Download the QR as PNG, SVG, or JSON. Print it and store it securely.
  6. Optionally back up to Google Drive from the result page.
  7. Your mnemonic never leaves your browser — no data is sent to any server.

Open Wallet

How do I decrypt my QR code to recover my mnemonic?
  1. Go to "Open Wallet" from the top menu.
  2. Scan your encrypted QR code using your camera, or upload the QR image / paste the data.
  3. Enter your password.
  4. W2QR decrypts the data in your browser.
  5. Your mnemonic is displayed on screen. Copy it, view wallet details, or open in the built-in wallet.
  6. The mnemonic exists in browser memory only during this session — it is not stored anywhere.

Wallet & Compatibility

Do I need a wallet app like MetaMask or Coinomi?
  1. No. W2QR works as a standalone "QR code app" — no external wallet software is required.
  2. After decrypting your seed phrase, you can use the built-in W2QR wallet to view addresses, check balances, and manage assets.
  3. You can also import the decrypted seed phrase into any BIP-39 compatible wallet: MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Coinomi, Rabby, Exodus, Ledger Live, Trezor Suite, and many others.
  4. W2QR encrypts the seed phrase itself, which is blockchain-agnostic — the same QR works for Ethereum, Bitcoin, Solana, and any BIP-39 chain.
Which QR formats does W2QR support?
  1. V1 — Legacy format from text2qr.com (AES-256-CBC, CryptoJS). Read-only.
  2. V2 — Current default. Honey encryption with Argon2id + XOR masking.
  3. W2QR automatically detects the format when you scan any QR code. All legacy backups are fully supported.

Wallet Extensions

How do I use the W2QR Wallet (standalone)?
  1. Install the W2QR Remote Signer Chrome extension.
  2. Open the extension popup and click "Import Account".
  3. Scan your encrypted QR code with the camera, or load it from a file (QR image or backup file).
  4. Decryption and key derivation happen entirely inside the extension — nothing is sent to any server.
  5. Choose a session duration (5 min to 1 hour) and click Unlock.
  6. Open any dApp — select "W2QR Remote Signer" when prompted for a wallet.
  7. Sign transactions as usual. Session expires automatically after your chosen duration.
How do I use W2QR with MetaMask?
  1. Go to "Tools > Connect Wallet" from the top menu and expand MetaMask.
  2. Install MetaMask Flask (the developer version that supports Snaps).
  3. Click "Connect to MetaMask" and approve the W2QR Snap installation.
  4. Scan your encrypted QR code and enter your password.
  5. A signing key is derived from your mnemonic and sent to the Snap.
  6. Your mnemonic is immediately cleared from memory — only the derived key remains.
  7. MetaMask now treats W2QR as an external signer, like a Ledger or Trezor.
  8. When a dApp requests a signature, MetaMask routes it through the W2QR Snap.
  9. After the session timeout (5 min to 1 hour), the key is cleared. Re-scan to sign again.
How do I use W2QR with Rabby?
  1. Install the W2QR Remote Signer Chrome extension — Rabby stays enabled.
  2. Open the W2QR extension popup, scan your QR code or load it from a file, and enter your password.
  3. Both Rabby and W2QR Remote Signer announce via EIP-6963 — dApps see both wallets.
  4. Select the W2QR provider when you need QR-protected signing; use Rabby for regular accounts.
  5. W2QR never intercepts or modifies Rabby — each wallet operates independently.
  6. Session expires automatically — re-import to sign again.

Security & Privacy

Is my data safe? What does the server know about me?
  1. Your mnemonic, private keys, and password never leave your browser.
  2. All cryptographic operations (Argon2id, XOR masking) happen client-side in WebAssembly.
  3. Google Drive backups are encrypted ciphertext — Google stores the file, but cannot read the contents without your password.
  4. The server never receives your password, mnemonic, private keys, or wallet addresses.
What happens if the W2QR server goes down?
  1. Wallet creation and decryption do not require the server — your QR + password is self-contained.
  2. Google Drive backups remain accessible directly through Google. Restoring requires your password.
  3. The server handles paid features (Non-Zero Balances Scanner) and is not needed for core encrypt/decrypt operations.

Find Non-Zero Balances

What is the Non-Zero Balances Scanner?
  1. Every time you unlock a wallet, W2QR checks the 100 most likely network / asset / derivation-path combinations for free.
  2. The Scanner lets you go beyond that: you choose the networks, assets, derivation-path patterns and index ranges yourself.
  3. The app counts exactly how many provider requests your search needs and shows the price before you pay anything.
  4. You pay in crypto from the wallet you just unlocked, then the scan runs and produces a summary plus a downloadable report.
How much does a scan cost?
  1. Your first 100 requests every 24 hours are free — that is the automatic scan that runs when you unlock.
  2. Beyond that, scanning costs $0.002 per request, with a $1.00 minimum per paid scan.
  3. One request covers one address across every EVM network you selected — adding more EVM networks does not cost more.
  4. Requests above 25,000 in a single scan are billed at $0.001.
  5. Estimating is always free and unlimited. Configure, estimate, adjust, estimate again — you only pay when you press Pay.
How do I pay, and in what?
  1. The price is shown in USDT. You can pay in USDT, USDC, or the native token of Polygon, BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Avalanche or Ethereum.
  2. Polygon is the default because network fees there are a fraction of a cent.
  3. Ethereum is available, but on a small scan the Ethereum network fee can cost more than the scan itself — the app shows you the fee next to each option.
  4. You pay directly from the wallet you unlocked, in one tap. The address that pays is the address the scan belongs to — no account or email is needed.
  5. If you overpay, the difference stays as balance on your address and never expires.
I lost one word of my seed phrase. Can W2QR find my wallet?
  1. Yes, if you have 11 of 12 words or 23 of 24 words. Fewer than that is not supported in this version.
  2. Enter the words you have. If you know which position is missing, say so — it makes the search dramatically cheaper.
  3. The BIP-39 checksum does most of the work: for a 24-word phrase with a known missing position there are usually only about 8 valid completions; for a 12-word phrase, about 135.
  4. W2QR enumerates every valid completion in your browser, derives their addresses, and scans them all.
  5. You see the exact number of candidates and the exact price before you pay — nothing here is an estimate.
Does my seed phrase leave my browser during a recovery scan?
  1. No. Candidate phrases are generated and their keys derived entirely in your browser, in a background worker.
  2. Only the resulting public addresses are sent to our server for balance lookup.
  3. The link between an address and the phrase that produced it exists only in your browser's memory and is destroyed when you lock the wallet or close the page.
  4. The report containing a recovered phrase is written in your browser and downloaded directly. We never receive it and never store it.
What happens if the scan finds nothing?
  1. The payment is not refunded. You are paying for the search, not for the result — this is stated before you pay.
  2. The results page shows exactly what was searched and what was not, so you can see where to look next.
  3. It suggests the next-widest search with its price, so you can decide whether to go further.
  4. If the scan fails for a technical reason on our side, the full amount is returned to your balance automatically.
What do I do the moment a lost phrase is recovered?
  1. Treat the recovered phrase as compromised the instant it exists in a file on your computer.
  2. Create a brand-new wallet, with a phrase you generate yourself, and protect it with W2QR.
  3. Move every asset from the recovered wallet to the new one.
  4. Delete the report file, and empty your downloads folder and trash.
  5. Download the redacted version of the report if you want a record of what was found without the phrase in it.

W2QR Cards

What are W2QR Cards?
  1. Physical or digital cards with pre-printed QR codes that become wallets when you activate them with your password.
  2. Each card contains honey-encrypted data — any password applied to it produces a valid BIP-39 mnemonic.
  3. The card QR is generated before any wallet exists. Your password choice determines which wallet you get.
  4. Cards follow the CR80/ID-1 standard (credit card size) and can be customized with templates and nicknames.
How do I create a card?
  1. Go to Create Card, choose a design template, add a nickname and wallet code, then print or download your card.
  2. The QR code is generated automatically using honey encryption — it works with any password.
  3. You can print the card at home or save it as an image for digital use.
How do I activate a card?
  1. Scan the card QR code (or paste the data), then enter your chosen password.
  2. The password combined with the card data creates your unique wallet mnemonic.
  3. You can optionally back up to Google Drive from the wallet tools.
Does creating a card affect my existing QR?
  1. No. Cards are independent — each card creates a new wallet.
  2. Your existing QR codes continue to work exactly as before.

Plastic Cards

What is a plastic card?
  1. A plastic card is a pre-printed QR card with honey encryption — credit-card-sized, durable, and easy to carry.
  2. You can create separate plastic cards for your wallet QR and your password QR — two cards, two locations. Together they open your wallet; apart, each is useless to an attacker.
  3. You activate a wallet card by scanning the QR and choosing a password.
  4. Once activated, you can optionally back up to Google Drive.
  5. The physical card stays valid. The printed QR does not change during activation.
Is a plastic card less secure than creating a wallet from scratch?
  1. A plastic card uses the same honey encryption as any wallet created in W2QR (QR + password).
  2. The only difference is the origin: a plastic card starts with a pre-generated mnemonic. The encryption and security properties are identical.
Can I still use the plastic card after activation?
  1. Yes. The original QR printed on the card still works — QR + password decrypts the mnemonic as before.
  2. Any backups you create (Google Drive) are additional copies. The printed card remains usable.

Account & Pricing

Is W2QR free?
  1. All core features are free: create and restore encrypted QR codes, back up to Google Drive, use the built-in wallet, check balances, and send transactions.
  2. The only paid feature is the Non-Zero Balances Scanner — a tool for widening the address search or recovering a wallet from a partial seed phrase.
  3. Every wallet unlock includes a free automatic scan of the 100 most likely combinations. Beyond that, scanning is priced per request and paid in crypto.
  4. No subscriptions, no premium plans. You pay only for the scans you run.
